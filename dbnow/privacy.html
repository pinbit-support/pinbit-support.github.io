dB Now — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

dB Now is a sound level meter for your phone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version: dB Now uses the microphone to measure how loud things are, and never records, stores or sends the sound. Your settings, saved checks and noise logs stay on your phone. We don't have accounts, analytics or a server. The free version shows ads from Google, which collects some device data to show them. dB Now Pro removes the ads.

The microphone

dB Now listens only to work out sound levels: while the meter is open on your screen, and during a night log (dB Now Pro) that you start, which keeps measuring with the screen off until it ends or you stop it. While a night log runs, your phone shows an ongoing notification with a Stop button, and dB Now reads the battery level on the phone so it can stop and save the log at 5 %; that isn't stored or sent. The sound is turned into numbers in the phone's memory as it arrives and then discarded. No audio is ever recorded, saved to storage or sent anywhere.

You can turn off dB Now's microphone access at any time in your phone's settings; the app then shows no reading.

What stays on your phone

dB Now stores these only on your device:

  • your settings, and your calibration offsets and profiles
  • the checks you save: when, how long, the minimum, average and maximum level, and one level a second for the check's graph
  • night logs (dB Now Pro): one level and one peak for each second, the place name you type, any notes you add to a loud moment, the time zone, the phone model, which microphone mode was used, and why the log ended

We never receive this information and have no way to see it. There are no accounts, no cloud sync and no analytics or crash-reporting services. dB Now never asks for your location: a log's place is only the name you give it. Measuring, calibrating and logging all work with no internet connection.

Threshold alerts (dB Now Pro) are notifications your phone raises itself, silently, when a level you set is passed. Nothing is sent to a server.

Sharing and exports: a saved check or a night log leaves your phone only when you tap Share or Export, through your phone's share sheet, to the place you choose.

Deleting it: delete a saved check or a night log from the History screen, or reset calibration from the Calibration screen. Uninstalling dB Now deletes everything it stored.

Ads (free version only)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: one banner at the bottom of the Meter and History screens, and now and then, after you save a check, one full-screen ad (an image or a video) that you close. The ad software is muted, so videos play silently: dB Now's ads never make sound.

dB Now asks Google for non-personalised ads only. They're chosen from what's on screen and your general location, not from a profile of you, and dB Now never asks to track you across other apps.

To show ads, count them and stop fraud, Google may still collect:

  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • information about the ads you see and tap
  • diagnostic information about how the ads perform

dB Now never gives the ad software any sound, measurement or log.

In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks before any ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Ad privacy choices in dB Now's Settings.

Google's use of this information is covered by Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

dB Now Pro removes all ads, and the ad software does not start at all for Pro users.

Purchases

dB Now Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive confirmation that you bought Pro, never your payment details. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies cover the purchase.

Children

dB Now is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
