Thockboard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Thockboard is a keyboard for your iPhone that looks the way you design it and sounds and feels like a mechanical keyboard. It comes with an app where you build your boards. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use them.

The short version: what you type never leaves your iPhone. The keyboard itself never connects to the internet. The app sends anonymous usage counts so we can see what works and fix what doesn't. These are tied to a random code, never to your name, email or Apple ID. There are no accounts and no ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your iPhone

  • What you type. The keyboard never sends, stores or shows us your typing.
  • Learned words. To suggest and correct words, the keyboard learns the words you use. They stay on your iPhone, are kept out of iCloud and computer backups, and you can delete them in Settings › Reset keyboard dictionary.
  • Your contacts' names. Like other keyboards, iOS can give the keyboard your contacts' names so it doesn't correct them. These are used on the phone, in memory only, and never stored or sent.
  • Your boards, photos and settings. The boards you build, any photo you put behind your keys and your settings are kept in the app on your iPhone.

Full Access

iOS asks whether to "Allow Full Access" for any keyboard that does more than type. Thockboard needs it to play key sounds and haptics. With Full Access on, the keyboard also keeps daily counts on your iPhone, which the app sends with its usage counts (below):

  • on how many days you typed with Thockboard, and roughly how many keys you pressed (in ranges such as "51–500 a week")
  • which of your boards, switches, keycaps and themes you typed with
  • how well the keyboard ran: freezes, sounds that failed to start, memory warnings and unexpected exits

These counts never include what you typed: no letters, words, timings of individual keys, or which app you were typing in. Without Full Access the keyboard still types; it just has no sound or haptics and keeps no counts.

Usage counts the app sends

When you use the app, it sends events such as "opened the app", "tried a theme", "saved a board", "saw the offer" or "started a free trial", together with:

  • a random install code made on your iPhone the first time the app opens (it's new if you reinstall, and it isn't linked to your name, email, Apple ID or any advertising identifier)
  • your iPhone's model and iOS version, the app's version, and your App Store country
  • whether you have Pro and which plan, how many boards you've saved, and whether the keyboard is set up
  • the keyboard's daily counts described above
  • basic error reports if the app or keyboard crashes or freezes (the kind of problem and where in our code, never your content)

These go to PostHog, our analytics service, hosted in the European Union. PostHog doesn't keep your IP address and doesn't look up your location. We use the counts only to improve Thockboard: to see where people get stuck, which designs people love and whether the keyboard works well on each kind of iPhone. We keep them for up to 7 years.

The app also downloads a small settings file from our website (pinbit-support.github.io, hosted by GitHub) that turns tests of new screens on or off. As with any web request, GitHub sees your IP address when it serves it.

Purchases

Thockboard Pro is sold through the Apple App Store; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat (in the United States) to keep a record of purchases under your random install code, such as which plan, when it started, renewed or ended, and the price in your currency. RevenueCat also tells us whether an install came from an Apple Search Ads campaign, using Apple's own privacy-preserving attribution; this needs no tracking permission and identifies no one. Apple's and RevenueCat's privacy policies cover their part.

Notifications and photos

  • Notifications: if you allow them, the reminder before a free trial ends is scheduled on your iPhone.
  • Photos and camera: only when you choose a photo or take one to put behind your keys, or save a picture of your board. Thockboard sees only that photo.

Your choices and rights

  • Uninstalling Thockboard deletes everything it stored on your iPhone, including the install code.
  • To ask us to delete the usage counts and purchase records tied to your install code, or for a copy of them, or to object to their use, email us. We may need the install code to find them; we can tell you how to look it up.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the UK, Switzerland, Korea or Japan), you may have further rights under data protection law, including to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

We process the usage counts because we have a legitimate interest in keeping Thockboard working well and improving it, and purchase records to provide what you bought. We don't sell or share your information for advertising, and we don't use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Where your information goes

PostHog stores the usage counts in the EU, and RevenueCat stores purchase records in the US. Both act on our instructions under data processing terms that protect your information to the standard of this policy, including when it crosses borders.

Children

Thockboard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
