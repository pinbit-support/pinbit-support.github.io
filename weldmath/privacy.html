Weld Math: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Weld Math is a welding calculator: weld settings, heat input, preheat, filler metal and weld cost. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version: your jobs, saved settings and calculations stay on your phone. We don't have accounts, analytics or a server. The free version shows ads from Google, which collects some device data to show them. Weld Math Pro removes the ads.

What stays on your phone

Weld Math stores these only on your device:

  • the jobs you create, and the weld settings and calculations saved in them
  • your unit choice, inches or millimeters
  • whether you have bought Weld Math Pro

We never receive this information and have no way to see it. There are no accounts, no cloud sync and no analytics or crash-reporting services. All the calculators, the reference tables and your jobs work with no internet connection.

Deleting it: delete a job, or one saved calculation, from Jobs. Uninstalling Weld Math deletes everything it stored.

Ads (free version only)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the home and reference screens. Ads never appear while you're using Weld settings or a calculator.

Weld Math asks Google for non-personalised ads only. They're chosen from what's on screen and your general location, not from a profile of you, and Weld Math never asks to track you across other apps.

To show ads, count them and stop fraud, Google may still collect:

  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • information about the ads you see and tap
  • diagnostic information about how the ads perform

In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks before any ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Ad privacy choices on the home screen.

Google's use of this information is covered by Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Weld Math Pro removes all ads, and the ad software does not start at all for Pro users.

Purchases

Weld Math Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive confirmation that you bought Pro, never your payment details. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies cover the purchase.

Children

Weld Math is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
